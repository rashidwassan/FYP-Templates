Ishara · PSL

Learn Pakistan Sign Language

Lessons

Short lessons from greetings to health, food and school. Each sign is shown as an animated schematic: handshape, place and movement.

Practice with your camera

The camera finds your hand, reads which fingers are extended and checks them against the target handshape. Hold the shape for a second to pass.

0.0 s

Finger-state matching checks the handshape only, not location or movement. A full sign-recognition model is a student extension.

Quiz

Spaced repetition: signs you miss come back sooner, signs you know come back later.

Dictionary

Search in English or Urdu, filter by category or handshape. Tap a sign to see the full description, practise it or suggest a correction.

Urdu fingerspelling

One handshape per Urdu letter, for names and words that have no sign yet. Learn the alphabet, then play the spelling game.

Sentence builder

PSL has its own grammar: time first, topic before comment, question signs at the end, and meaning carried by the face. Put the sign tiles in PSL order.

Conversations

Everyday dialogues built from the signs you are learning: a doctor visit, the market, school and a family visit.

Progress

XP, streaks, mastery per category, practice analytics and badges.

Classroom

Assignments for your class, local student codes and a progress board. Everything stays on this device; students send their progress file to the teacher.

Settings

Language, display, practice, daily goals, student profile and your data.
