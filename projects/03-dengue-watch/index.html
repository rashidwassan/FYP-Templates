Report a breeding site

Aedes breeds in clean standing water: coolers, drums, tyres, plant saucers. Pin it, photograph it, get it cleared.

Campaign planner

Schedule fogging, larviciding, awareness walks, school sessions and clean-up drives. Upcoming activities also appear on the report map.

Weekly prevention

Ten minutes every week breaks the mosquito's 7–10 day life cycle. Pick a “dry day” and keep the streak.
